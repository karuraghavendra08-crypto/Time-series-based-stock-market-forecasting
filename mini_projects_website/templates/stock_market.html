Engineering Methodology

End-to-End Predictive Pipeline

Every step of data transformation enforces strict temporal causality to prevent forward-looking data leakage.

STEP 01

Data Ingestion

Acquires 27,824 historical Dow Jones trading days and live Yahoo Finance real-time OHLCV candles without synthetic dates.

STEP 02

32 Technical Features

Computes momentum (RSI, Stochastic), trend (MACD, EMA ratios), volatility (Bollinger Bands, ATR), and return lags backward-only.

STEP 03

Chronological Split

70% Train / 15% Validation / 15% Test forward split. Zero random shuffling. Scalers fitted strictly on training data only.

STEP 04

Model Serialization

Pre-trained models persisted to saved_models/ (.joblib, .keras, .pkl) for instant millisecond inference.

STEP 05

Live REST Inference

Flask API serves real-time forward forecasts with verified return-to-price mathematics and plain-English breakdown.
