About the Forecasting Platform

This project demonstrates a production-grade approach to financial time-series forecasting. Unlike naive price regressions, it formulates market forecasting as a 5-day forward trend regime classification problem and multi-horizon return estimation.

The system implements 32 backward-looking technical indicators (zero look-ahead bias), 60-day chronological sequence modeling with Stacked LSTM & RNN networks, and gradient boosted regime classifiers.

All models are persisted on disk in saved_models/ and execute live inference against real-time market data streamed from Yahoo Finance.
